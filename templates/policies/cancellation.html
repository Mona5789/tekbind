Cancellation Policy

Last updated: {{ now|date:"F d, Y" }}

At Tekbind Technology Services, we strive to provide high-quality services and ensure customer satisfaction. This Cancellation Policy outlines the terms under which cancellations are accepted.

1. Cancellation Requests

Customers may request cancellation of services or orders by contacting us through our official communication channels.

  • Email: tekbindservices@gmail.com
  • Phone: +91-9663654114
  • Or through our Contact Us page

2. Cancellation Eligibility

  • Orders can be cancelled before the service or project work has been initiated.
  • Once the service has commenced, cancellation may not be possible.
  • For ongoing projects, cancellation requests will be reviewed on a case-by-case basis.

3. Timeframe for Cancellation

  • Cancellation requests should be made within 24–48 hours of placing the order.
  • Requests made after this period may not be eligible for cancellation.

4. Charges & Deductions

  • If cancellation is approved, administrative or processing charges may apply.
  • Work already completed will be charged proportionately.
  • Third-party costs (if any) will be deducted from the refund amount.

5. Non-Cancellable Services

  • Completed services are non-cancellable.
  • Customized or digital services once delivered cannot be cancelled.
  • Urgent or priority services once initiated cannot be cancelled.

6. Refund on Cancellation

If a cancellation is approved, any applicable refund will be processed as per our Refund Policy.

7. Company Rights

  • We reserve the right to cancel any order due to unforeseen circumstances.
  • In such cases, a full refund will be issued to the customer.

8. Changes to Policy

Tekbind Technology Services reserves the right to update or modify this Cancellation Policy at any time. Changes will be effective immediately upon posting on this page.
